Problem Statement & Objectives
Bridging first-principles physical equations with interactive telemetry
🚨 Engineering Problem
  • Simulating Solar EV performance on closed circuits requires accounting for competing physical limits: aerodynamic resistance, motor torque-power envelopes, tyre adhesion boundaries, and tight battery energy reserves.
  • Conventional dashboards frequently rely on synthetic or random metrics rather than genuine physics-integrated states.
  • High efficiency solar vehicles require precise energy audits (consumption, regenerative braking recovery, and solar PV irradiance gains).
🎯 Deliverable Objectives
  • Modular Physics Engine: Complete Newton-Euler longitudinal dynamics, PMSM motor limits, lithium battery equivalent circuit.
  • Kari Motor Speedway Track: Parametric spline model with continuous curvature calculation and sector partitioning.
  • Lap Velocity Solver: Forward acceleration and backward braking passes for a complete, feasible lap.
  • Telemetry Cockpit: Interactive dark-theme dashboard with animated vehicle positioning and telemetry export.

Mathematical & Physics Modelling
First-principles formulations implemented across the digital twin modules
Chassis & Powertrain Dynamics
  • Equation of Motion:
    $m \cdot a_x = F_{\text{drive}} - F_{\text{drag}} - F_{\text{rolling}} - F_{\text{braking}}$
  • Aerodynamic Resistance:
    $F_{\text{drag}} = \frac{1}{2} \rho C_d A v^2 \quad (\rho = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3)$
  • Rolling Resistance:
    $F_{\text{rolling}} = C_{rr} m g \cos(\theta)$
  • Motor Regimes:
    $T = T_{\text{max}}$ for $\omega \le \omega_{\text{base}}$;
    $T = P_{\text{max}} / \omega$ for $\omega > \omega_{\text{base}}$
Battery, Solar & Tyre Limits
  • Battery Equivalent Circuit:
    $V_{\text{term}} = V_{\text{ocv}}(SOC) - I \cdot R_{\text{int}}$
  • SOC Integration:
    $dSOC = - \frac{I \cdot dt}{Q_{\text{cap}} \cdot 3600} \times 100\%$
  • Solar PV Harvest:
    $P_{\text{solar}} = A_{pv} \eta_{pv} \eta_{\text{mppt}} G_{\text{solar}} \approx 725.5\text{ W}$
  • Friction Circle Grip Constraint:
    $(a_x / \mu g)^2 + (a_y / \mu g)^2 \le 1.0$
Kari Motor Speedway Track Modelling
Parametric spline geometry of the Coimbatore racing circuit
Circuit Geometry Specifications
  • Length: 2,120 meters (calibrated perimeter).
  • Turns: 14 corners, driven anti-clockwise.
  • Discretization: Uniform arc length $\Delta s = 2.0\text{ m}$ (1,060 discrete simulation nodes).
  • Analytical Curvature:
    $\kappa(s) = \frac{x' y'' - y' x''}{(x'^2 + y'^2)^{3/2}}$
  • Cornering Velocity Ceiling:
    $v_{\text{corner}}(s) = \sqrt{\frac{\mu g \cdot 0.98}{|\kappa(s)|}}$
Sector Partitioning
  • Sector 1 (0 to 678m)
    Start/Finish straight (500m), T1 & T2 fast sweeper, T3 entry.
  • Sector 2 (678 to 1441m)
    T5 Hairpin ($R \approx 22\text{ m}$ apex, slowest corner), T6-T7 chicane, infield complex.
  • Sector 3 (1441 to 2120m)
    Back sweeper kinks, T13-T14 parabolic curve back to main straight.

Results, Validation & Limitations
Energy audit, automated test coverage, and transparent engineering boundaries
⚡ Energy Audit (1 Flying Lap)
  • Gross Energy Consumed: $0.224\text{ kWh}$
  • Regenerative Braking Harvest: $0.016\text{ kWh}$ (~7.1% energy recovered)
  • Solar PV Harvest: $0.015\text{ kWh}$ ($725.5\text{ W}$ continuous gain)
  • Net Energy Consumed: 0.208 kWh
  • Specific Energy Efficiency: 98.3 Wh/km
  • Battery State of Charge: 90.00% $\to$ 85.55% ($\Delta = -4.45\%$)
✅ Unit Tests & Physical Constraints
  • 13/13 Automated Tests Passed:
    • Aerodynamic drag scales strictly with $v^2$ ($R^2 = 1.0$)
    • Motor torque and power capped at 75 Nm and 12 kW
    • SOC decrements on discharge, increments on regen
    • Terminal voltage sags proportionally to $I \cdot R_{\text{int}}$
    • Cornering speed decreases with increasing curvature
  • Documented Limitations: Planar elevation, quasi-steady state load transfer, constant battery temperature ($30^\circ\text{C}$).
